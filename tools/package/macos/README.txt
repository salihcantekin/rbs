RBS
===

A live production engine: capture, compose, encode, stream and record.
License: GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE). Source: https://github.com/salihcantekin/rbs

Run it
------
Open the RBS folder and double-click RBS.app. The first time, macOS may say it cannot
verify the app: right-click RBS.app, choose Open, then Open again.

Notes
-----
- This is a portable download, not an installer. Your profiles, scene collections
  and settings are kept in:
    ~/Library/Application Support/RBS
  Copy that folder to back up your setup.
- The browser source needs its engine (about 200 MB): download it once in
  Settings > Add-ons.
- AI tools: the program "rbs-mcp" next to RBS is started from Settings > AI tools.
